## 1. Executive Summary & Mission

The **Tawjihi Programme** is an Algerian-led initiative delivered under **We Are Still Here**, the grassroots educational project founded in the United Kingdom by **Prof. Zahid Pranjol** to support displaced and crisis-affected students in Gaza. 

Teaching is designed, staffed, and coordinated by qualified Algerian volunteer teachers, working directly to the **official Palestinian Tawjihi curriculum** while drawing upon the pedagogical rigor and depth of the Algerian Baccalaureate experience. This document sets out the teaching structure for the Tawjihi Division: how subject-level teaching is organised, supervised, and reported on to ensure academic excellence and uninterrupted delivery.

## 3. Tiered Roles & Accountabilities

### Level 1: Founder & Strategic Oversight (Prof. Zahid Pranjol)
* **Leadership Role:** Global vision, strategic direction, and policy formulation for the We Are Still Here initiative.
* **Key Responsibilities:**
  * Setting institutional priorities and maintaining UK/international stakeholder partnerships.
  * Direct supervision of the three Teaching Coordinators.
  * Maintaining a dotted-line relationship with all Lead Teachers for direct strategic feedback on frontline teaching quality.

### Level 2: The Three Teaching Coordinators
* **Composition:** **Exactly three senior Teaching Coordinators** (e.g., Nasreddine Drissi, Anissa Haddadi, and 3rd Coordinator).
* **Scope of Authority:** **Horizontal, cross-subject coordination** across disciplines.
* **Key Responsibilities:**
  * Overseeing portfolio groupings of subjects and ensuring balanced academic loads.
  * Master timetable scheduling: completely eliminating scheduling conflicts between different subjects for each cohort.
  * Cohort management: defining class capacities, grouping students into tracks (Scientific, Literary, Azhari), and approving roster transfers.
  * Operational escalation: addressing cross-subject logistics, parent/student communications, and system-wide attendance patterns.
  * Aggregating performance and operational data for regular executive reporting to Prof. Zahid Pranjol.

### Level 3: Lead Teachers — One per Subject
* **Composition:** One dedicated subject lead for each Tawjihi subject (English, Physics, Mathematics, Arabic, Islamic Studies, etc.).
* **Reporting Lines:** Reports directly to one assigned Teaching Coordinator (solid line) with strategic access to the Founder (dotted line).
* **Core Mandate:**
  > **«To keep teaching running without interruption»**
  1. **Session Confirmation:** Verifying that scheduled classes take place reliably and on time.
  2. **Arranging Cover:** Instantly deploying qualified substitute teachers whenever a volunteer teacher is unavailable, ensuring zero lost learning hours for Gaza students.
  3. **Curriculum Pacing & Planning:** Structuring session plans ahead of time aligned with Palestinian Ministry standards.
  4. **Resource Distribution:** Guaranteeing that textbooks, exercise sheets, and question banks reach students systematically.
  5. **Pedagogical Quality:** Observing sessions, mentoring volunteer teachers, and auditing assessment standards.

### Level 4: Volunteer Teachers
* **Composition:** Qualified Algerian volunteer educators with proven secondary/Baccalaureate expertise.
* **Key Responsibilities:** Delivering interactive live classes, tracking daily attendance, offering student mentorship, and grading assignments and diagnostic exams.

## 4. Role Comparison: Teaching Coordinator vs. Lead Teacher

| Evaluation Dimension | Teaching Coordinator | Lead Teacher |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operational Axis** | **Horizontal:** Cross-subject harmonization. | **Vertical:** Deep domain and subject expertise. |
| **Staffing Quota** | **Exactly 3 Coordinators** across the entire division. | **One dedicated Lead Teacher per subject**. |
| **Core Accountability** | Schedule cohesion, cohort capacity, and logistics. | **Zero interruption: session delivery & instant cover.** |
| **Cohort Interaction** | Creates cohorts, sets size caps, enrolls students. | Manages teacher assignments within their subject. |
| **Direct Reporting** | Reports directly to Prof. Zahid Pranjol. | Reports to assigned Coordinator (dotted line to Founder). |

## 5. Decision & Governance Matrix (RACI)

* **A (Accountable):** Sole decision-maker with final ownership.
* **R (Responsible):** Direct executor tasked with delivery.
* **C (Consulted):** Consulted for specialized input prior to action.
* **I (Informed):** Kept updated on progress and outcomes.

| Operational Function | Founder (Prof. Zahid) | 3 Coordinators | Lead Teacher | Volunteer Teacher |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Strategic Direction & Division Policies** | **A** | C | I | I |
| **Appointment of Coordinators & Lead Teachers** | **A** | R / C | I | I |
| **Timetable Synchronization & Conflict Prevention** | I | **A / R** | C | I |
| **Cohort Formation & Student Enrollment Limits** | I | **A / R** | C | I |
| **Arranging Instant Teacher Cover (Contingency)** | I | C | **A / R** | C |
| **Curriculum Plan Distribution & Resource Delivery** | I | C | **A / R** | C |
| **Exam Standard Validation & Question Banking** | I | I | **A / R** | R |
| **Live Session Delivery & Attendance Auditing** | I | I | C | **A / R** |
| **Pedagogical Performance & Teaching Quality Review** | C | C | **A / R** | I |
